About this project

Segmentation here means grouping songs by playlist genre and subgenre, then comparing their audio features. The dataset has playlist entries, so a track on several playlists appears more than once.

Built with HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. Chart.js draws the charts and Papa Parse reads the CSV in your browser.
